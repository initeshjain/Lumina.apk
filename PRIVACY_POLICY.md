# Privacy Policy for Lumina

**Last Updated:** November 27, 2026

Lumina ("we", "our", or "the application") is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains our practices regarding user information.

---

### 1. Zero Data Collection & Offline Architecture
- **Completely Offline:** Lumina does not request or possess the Android Internet permission (`android.permission.INTERNET`). The application cannot connect to the internet, cloud servers, or third-party networks.
- **No Personal Information Collected:** We do not collect, store, track, monitor, or transmit any personally identifiable information (PII), identifiers, advertising IDs, or telemetry data.
- **No Analytics or Trackers:** Lumina contains no third-party tracking SDKs, analytical libraries, crash-reporting services, or advertisement frameworks.

---

### 2. Device Storage & Media Access
To function as an on-device gallery and media organizer, Lumina requests Android storage permissions:
- `READ_MEDIA_IMAGES` & `READ_MEDIA_VIDEO` (Android 13+)
- `READ_EXTERNAL_STORAGE` (Android 12 and below)

**How these permissions are used:**
- Accessing photos and videos stored locally on your device to display them in albums, timelines, and viewer interfaces.
- Allowing you to organize, batch delete, move, or clean up files at your explicit instruction.
- **Your media files never leave your device.** All scanning, sorting, and image processing operations occur strictly in local memory.

---

### 3. Hidden Vault & Security
- The Hidden Vault feature allows you to isolate sensitive photos behind a local PIN code.
- Your PIN code is securely hashed on your device and never transmitted off the device.
- Trashed items remain safely in your device storage until you choose to permanently delete them or restore them.

---

### 4. Third-Party Sharing
Since Lumina has no internet access and no remote backend servers, we do not share, sell, lease, or distribute your photos, videos, or usage data to any third party under any circumstances.

---

### 5. Changes to This Privacy Policy
We may occasionally update this Privacy Policy. Any updates will be reflected by revising the "Last Updated" date at the top of this document.
